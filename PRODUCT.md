# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chinese-speaking users and project operators who use a medical knowledge graph QA system to ask symptom, disease, treatment, food, drug, and checkup questions. Admin users also inspect retrieval and debug details while tuning the RAG pipeline.

## Product Purpose

Provide a focused medical QA interface backed by Neo4j knowledge graph retrieval and LLM generation, reducing hallucination by grounding answers in retrieved structured medical facts. Success means users can ask a natural medical question, choose the model and retrieval range, and receive a clear answer with minimal interface friction.

## Brand Personality

Calm, professional, efficient. The interface should feel familiar like a modern chat tool, but restrained enough for medical information work.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style landing pages, decorative medical stock imagery, dense control panels that compete with the chat, and playful diagnosis-style visuals that would reduce trust.

## Design Principles

- Put the chat workflow first.
- Make retrieval controls understandable without exposing internal jargon.
- Keep visual styling quiet and consistent so answers and debug evidence remain readable.
- Preserve keyboard efficiency for repeated question asking.
- Treat medical context with careful, non-alarming language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, readable Chinese text, and reduced-motion handling for non-essential animation.
